Elon Reeve Musk is a business magnate and investor. 
He was born on June 28, 1971, in Pretoria, South Africa. 
Musk is known for founding SpaceX, Tesla, Neuralink, and The Boring Company. 
He also co-founded Zip2 and X.com, which later became PayPal. 
Currently, he owns X Corp., which was formerly known as Twitter. 
Musk immigrated to Canada before moving to the United States. 
He studied at Queen's University and later transferred to the University of Pennsylvania, 
where he earned degrees in economics and physics.
